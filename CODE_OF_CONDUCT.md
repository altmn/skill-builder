# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and observable behavior without harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or disclosure of private information. Use synthetic examples and respect others' ownership and licensing.

Alex Allen (@altmn) maintains this project and may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation. Enforcement follows the circumstances; no response-time commitment is made.

For a non-sensitive conduct concern, open an issue requesting a confidential contact route without naming affected people or publishing details. Do not use public issues for private allegations. Security vulnerabilities belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). GitHub's own reporting and blocking tools remain available under its policies.
