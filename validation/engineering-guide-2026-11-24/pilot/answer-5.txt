In YAML, `>` starts a **folded string**. YAML joins consecutive lines of ordinary text with spaces, so you can wrap a long string in the file while keeping it on one line in the parsed value.

```yaml
message: >
  Your order is ready.
  Pick it up at the front desk.
```

The parsed value is `"Your order is ready. Pick it up at the front desk.\n"`. A blank line between paragraphs produces a newline, and line breaks around more-indented text are preserved.

The marker also controls trailing newlines:

| Marker | End of the parsed string |
|---|---|
| `>` | Keeps the final newline, if present, and drops trailing blank lines |
| `>-` | Removes the final newline and any trailing blank lines |
| `>+` | Keeps the final newline and any trailing blank lines |

Use `|` for a **literal string** when the line breaks inside the text should stay exactly where you put them.
